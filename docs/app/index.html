How sites are counted
  • Data: all MERMAID surveys with a public or public summary data policy. Private data are not included.
  • Benthic combines the PIT, LIT and PQT protocols.
  • Years: a site's count is the number of distinct calendar years with data.
  • Pairing: with several protocols ticked, surveys are paired when every ticked protocol was surveyed at the site within the pairing window of any one of those surveys (so the outermost surveys can be up to twice the window apart). Each paired visit counts in the year of its earliest survey. Protocols that are not ticked are ignored.
  • Sites, not surveys: counts here are sites. MERMAID Explore counts surveys, so its totals are higher.
  • Sites are matched by MERMAID site record, so the same reef recorded under two projects appears twice.

The full method, with the same rules worked through in R, is in the Quarto document.
